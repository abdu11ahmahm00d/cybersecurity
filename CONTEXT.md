# Context

The vocabulary of this effort. One canonical meaning per term. If a word is used differently from its
entry here, the entry wins until we deliberately change it.

## Career and market

- **Role family** — a cluster of jobs sharing a skill base: *security operations*, *offensive security*,
  *GRC (governance, risk, compliance)*, *appsec*, *forensics/IR*, *security engineering*, *security research*.
  A role family is not a job title. "Cybersecurity" alone names none of them.
- **Offensive security** — work that finds and exploits weaknesses with authorisation: penetration testing,
  red teaming, exploit development, bug bounty.
- **Defensive security** — work that detects, contains, and prevents: SOC analysis, incident response,
  threat hunting, detection engineering, hardening, GRC.
- **Blue team / red team / purple team** — the defensive, offensive, and integrated-collaboration framings of
  the same organisation's security function. Purple is a workflow, not a third job market.
- **Entry-level** — a role a fresh graduate can hold with no prior professional security employment:
  typically SOC tier 1, junior GRC/compliance, IT-security support, junior appsec, junior pentest in a
  consultancy that trains.
- **Certificate track / ladder** — the ordered set of vendor and vendor-neutral certifications that a market
  actually filters on, cheapest and most foundational first. A ladder is *market-specific*: the certs that
  open doors in Dhaka are not the certs that open doors in Berlin.

## Certification names (disambiguation in progress)

- **PT1** — as used by the friend in the brief. *Not yet pinned*: candidates are TCM Security's PJPT/PNPT
  line, CompTIA PenTest+ (exam code PT0-00x), INE's eJPT, and OffSec's OSCP. Treated as ambiguous until
  the ticket resolves it.
- **CEH** — EC-Council Certified Ethical Hacker. Multiple-choice, theory-heavy, widely known by name,
  widely criticised by practitioners for not proving hands-on ability.
- **OSCP** — OffSec Certified Professional. 24-hour hands-on exam; the long-standing practitioner benchmark
  for offensive work.
- **CompTIA Security+ / CySA+** — vendor-neutral entry and analyst-tier certifications; the common
  check-box for government and bank hiring.

## Learning

- **Fundamentals** — networking (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP), operating systems (Linux and Windows internals),
  programming (Python plus one of C/Go), and cryptography basics. Every role family sits on top of these;
  none of them can be skipped by choosing a "track".
- **Home lab** — the local environment (VMs, containers, vulnerable-by-design machines) where practice
  happens without permission problems.
- **CTF** — capture-the-flag: a scored exercise, not a job simulation.

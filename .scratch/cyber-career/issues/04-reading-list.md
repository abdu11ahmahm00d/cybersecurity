# The 8-book reading list

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Pick eight books a beginner can actually finish, that together cover the ground from first principles to
employable skill, prioritising the newest credible editions (2024–2026 where they exist). For each book:
title, authors, edition and year, what it is for, what it assumes, where it sits in a reading order, and
one honest caveat.

Constraints:

- Mix: at least one networking/OS foundation text, at least one hands-on offensive text, at least one
  defensive/blue-team text, at least one web/appsec text, at least one that explains the *profession*
  (career, ethics, law), and — because of the owner's data-science background — at least one on AI/ML
  security or detection with data.
- No filler: reject books that are certification dumps or vendor brochures.
- State which are available legitimately in Bangladesh (print, Kindle, or library) and which are
  hard to obtain, with the cheapest legitimate route.
- Give a reading order with rough weekly pacing for a student carrying a full course load.

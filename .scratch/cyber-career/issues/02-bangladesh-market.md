# Bangladesh cybersecurity job market: what is actually hired, at what level, for how much

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

Build the market picture the career decision hinges on, with sources and dates:

1. Which employers in Bangladesh hire security staff — banks and financial institutions (and their
   regulator-driven requirements), telecoms, the government (BGD e-GOV CIRT, Digital Security Agency),
   BPO/IT services, the garments sector's compliance pressure, telco vendors, regional SOCs serving foreign
   clients, and local consultancies.
2. **Offensive vs defensive**: how many actual openings of each exist, and what the split means for a
   graduate. Is the friend's claim that "Bangladesh has few offensive jobs" true in 2026, and is it because
   of compliance culture, because offensive work is exported, or because there are only a handful of firms?
3. Entry-level reality: what titles exist (SOC analyst L1, VAPT trainee, GRC associate, IT security officer),
   what they require, and whether they hire fresh graduates with certificates but no experience.
4. Salary bands, stated honestly, with source (job postings, surveys, practitioner reports).
5. Remote/offshore work: can a Dhaka-based person get paid in foreign currency doing SOC or pentest work,
   and what does that path require?
6. Which certificates actually appear in Bangladeshi job postings — count them if possible.

# The real certificate ladder: what each cert proves, costs, and who filters on it

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

What is the actual certification landscape for someone entering cybersecurity, cheapest foundation first,
with for each certificate: issuing body, current price, exam format (multiple choice vs hands-on lab vs
report), how long it realistically takes a beginner, and — the part marketing pages hide — **who actually
filters on it**: employers in Bangladesh, employers abroad, and university admissions.

Specifically resolve:

1. Is CEH "rudimentary", as the friend claims? What does it prove, what does it cost, and is it still a
   hiring filter anywhere that matters (government, banks, Bangladesh)?
2. What is "PT1"? Enumerate the candidates (TCM PJPT/PNPT, CompTIA PenTest+ PT0-003, INE eJPT, OffSec
   OSCP/PEN-200) and state which one a Bangladeshi student most plausibly means.
3. CompTIA Security+ and CySA+: price, exam format, and whether they are the realistic entry rung in
   Bangladesh or a formality.
4. ISC2 CC and CISSP, GIAC/SANS: where they sit, and why the price gap.
5. The vendor-neutral vs vendor track split, and which one a student with a small budget should buy first.

# Cybersecurity

Field notes, verified research, and study artifacts for a shift from data science into cybersecurity —
as a final-year major, and as a career track (Bangladesh first, abroad optional).

Nothing here is memorized advice. Every claim that reaches a page carries where it came from, and the
research that supports it lives in [`.scratch/`](.scratch/).

## Where things live

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |

| [`.scratch/cyber-career/issues/`](.scratch/cyber-career/issues/) | Decision tickets, one file each, with the answer at the bottom |

## How the map works

This repo is planned the way a large piece of work is charted: a destination, then decision tickets that
get resolved one at a time. A ticket is a *question whose answer is a decision*, not a task. When its
question is answered, the answer goes into the ticket, the ticket is marked resolved, and the one-line
gist lands on the map. The map is the index; the tickets hold the detail.
